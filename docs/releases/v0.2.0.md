# v0.2.0 — material schema, shared motion and USDZ

**Tagged:** 2026-10-05 ·
[Release page](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-mmd-plugins/releases/tag/v0.2.0)

The second release: the parts of Phases 8, 9 and 10 of
[DESIGN_POLICY.md §14](../design/DESIGN_POLICY.md#14-phases) that this
repository owes. What remains of each Phase is owned outside it, or waits on
another repository ([roadmap](../roadmap/current.md)).

## Objective

Give the PMX stage the contract an MMD-aware renderer reads, and hand MMD
motion to the shared motion core in both directions, without the importer
evaluating anything:

- **Phase 8, this repository's part:** `MmdMaterialAPI` on every material,
  its values connected into the portable fallbacks, and a UsdImaging adapter
  that shows them to Hydra; generic clips and connector captures retargeted
  onto a PMX, and the retargeted pose finished on the model's own helpers.
- **Phase 9:** MMD motion evaluated over the control rig and normalized into
  `usd-motion-plugins`' `MotionClip`, and the PMX skeleton described for
  generic retargeting, with the arm chain's reference rest stated on both
  sides.
- **Phase 10, steps 1–3:** a validated, deterministic USDZ that opens
  without this repository's plugins.

## What shipped

- **Stage-contract version 2** in **`usdMmdFileFormat`**: every material
  applies `MmdMaterialAPI`, its canonical values are Material interface
  inputs `inputs:mmd:material:*`, and `/preview` and `/mtlx` connect to them
  ([STAGE_CONTRACT.md §2](../design/STAGE_CONTRACT.md#2-contract-version)).
  The importer now requires the `mmdSchema` bundle at run time.
- **`mmdSchema`**, the `usd-schema` bundle that registers `MmdMaterialAPI`,
  with generated C++ accessors, also installed as a CMake package.
- **`mmdImaging`**, the `usd-imaging` bundle that exposes `MmdMaterialAPI` to
  Hydra as `mmd/material/<field>` and `mmd/drawOrder`
  ([MATERIAL_POLICY.md §12.1](../design/MATERIAL_POLICY.md#121-the-hydra-view-mmdimaging)).
- **`mmd_export`**, which packages a `.pmx` as a standard USDZ: BMP and TGA
  converted to PNG losslessly, byte-deterministic across time zones, and
  `--portable-paths` for ASCII archive names
  ([PACKAGING_POLICY.md](../design/PACKAGING_POLICY.md),
  [guide](../guides/exporting.md)).
- **`mmdControl`**, a plain library that evaluates a bound VMD over the
  control rig in MMD's order: Bézier tracks, bone and group morphs, appends,
  and IK. `Evaluator::Complete` finishes a pose given from elsewhere on the
  model's appends and IK
  ([MOTION_CONTRACT.md §11](../design/MOTION_CONTRACT.md#11-evaluating-the-control-rig)).
- **`mmdMotionAdapter`** and **`mmdSkeletonAdapter`**, the only edges to
  `usd-motion-plugins`: evaluated motion as a `MotionClip`, with root motion
  and namespaced morph channels; and the PMX skeleton as a
  `SkeletonDescriptor`, a `RetargetMap` from role-table version 2, the arm
  chain's reference rest as `SourceRestPose` and `TargetRestPose`, and
  `CarryArmRoll` and `heldJoints` for the finish
  ([MOTION_CONTRACT.md §10](../design/MOTION_CONTRACT.md#10-normalizing-into-the-shared-motion-core),
  [§12](../design/MOTION_CONTRACT.md#12-the-humanoid-role-table)).

The capabilities, feature by feature, are the
[capability matrix](../reference/CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) at this tag, and the
change by change detail is the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md). Local runs
over distributed models, motions, VRMA clips and recorded mocopi captures are
recorded in [reports/](../reports/).

## Compatibility

- **Stage-contract version:** 2, incompatible with 1: a reader of
  `mmd:material:<name>` reads `inputs:mmd:material:<name>` instead
  ([STAGE_CONTRACT.md §2](../design/STAGE_CONTRACT.md#2-contract-version)).
- **OpenUSD:** 26.08, exactly
  ([DEPENDENCIES.md §1](../architecture/DEPENDENCIES.md#1-openusd)).
- **`usd-motion-plugins`:** `>=0.5.3,<0.6`, verified against v0.5.3, for the
  adapters only; nothing else links it
  ([DEPENDENCIES.md §6](../architecture/DEPENDENCIES.md#6-usd-motion-plugins)).
- **Platforms:** Windows x86_64, macOS arm64, Linux x86_64, each as the
  OpenStrata `cy2026` / `usd` target with Python 3.13.
- **Packages:** every library, and `mmdSchema`, is version 0.2.0 with
  `SameMinorVersion` compatibility: `find_package(mmdModel 0.2 CONFIG
  REQUIRED)`. A consumer asking for 0.1 does not find 0.2.

## Artifacts

Per target: `usd-mmd-plugins-0.2.0-<target>-plugin-product.tar.zst` (the
three bundles and three tools); `usdMmdFileFormat-`, `mmdSchema-`,
`mmdImaging-`, `mmd_inspect-`, `vmd_inspect-` and
`mmd_export-0.2.0-<target>.tar.zst`; a `.manifest.json` for each. Once:
`usd-mmd-plugins-0.2.0-src.tar.gz` and `SHA256SUMS`. The libraries,
`mmdControl` and the adapters among them, build from the source archive.

## Known limitations at this release

- The importer still evaluates nothing. `mmdControl` and the adapters
  evaluate motion for a consumer that links them; nothing bakes a VMD into
  the stage, and physics is preserved but not simulated.
- `hydra-toon`'s MMD path, the avatar runtime's consumption of these
  packages, and the physics runtime are Phase 8 work owned outside this
  repository, and none has happened yet.
- Expressions stay MMD morph channels: the shared core has no common
  expression semantic yet.
- MOT-O11: whether a knee's IK starts from its keyed rotation awaits MMD's
  own output to compare against.
- `.vmd` does not open as a stage; `usdVmdFileFormat` waits for MOT-O2.
- A shared packaging layer (Phase 10 step 4) waits for a VRM packager.
- SDEF is skinned by linear blending (C, R0 and R1 preserved); QDEF is
  unverified. The portable material graphs remain unlit approximations.
- Display frames and PMX 2.1 soft bodies are parsed and not authored.
- One OpenUSD release.
