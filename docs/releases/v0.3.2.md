# v0.3.2 — real models with self-joints and bones several bodies drive

**Date:** 2026-11-01 ·
[Release page and final run identities](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-mmd-plugins/releases/tag/v0.3.2)

## Summary

Lets models that were refused run through
[`usd-avatar-runtime`'s MMD session](https://github.com/animu-sphere/usd-avatar-runtime/blob/main/docs/architecture/MMD_SESSION.md),
found running local models through it.

## What ships

- `usdMmdFileFormat` authors a joint from a rigid body to itself with
  `physics:jointEnabled = false`, and raises `MMD_PHYSICS_JOINT_SELF`.
- `mmdPhysicsAdapter` lets the latest body in source order drive a bone several simulated
  bodies name, and reports `MMD_PHYSICS_BONE_MULTIPLY_DRIVEN`, instead of refusing the binding.
- Every library, bundle, tool and the product, rebuilt at this version.

## Compatibility

- **Stage contract:** stays at 2. A disabled joint is a valid `UsdPhysics` prim. A self-joint
  needs `usd-physics-plugins` 0.1.2 or later to read a stage that has one.
- **Library API:** unchanged. Sibling ranges stay `>=0.3,<0.4`. A consumer that relied on
  `Bind` refusing several bodies per bone now gets a successful binding with a diagnostic.

## Accepted limitations

- The rule for several bodies per bone is this owner's and deterministic. It does not claim
  native MMD equivalence, and neither do the evaluation results above it.
- The limits of the [v0.3.1 record](v0.3.1.md) otherwise stand.
